Written 2026-10-12, built the same day: every clip, the float, Lunge's three clips, Canopy,
Raincheck (rules, rain, icon) and the tests are in the game; `AGENTS.md` §9 has the result
and the known contact. It records the agreed design, with the owner's answers to the open
questions in §6. Later notes from the owner, also built: the canopy shows the same panels
inside as out, the cape is the same both sides and lies on his back, the scarf is teal, and
his right arm rests at his side in every clip.

## 2. Model and rig changes before animating

1. **Delete the hobby horse:**
   - In the build: `build_horse`, `build_reins`, the `horse`, `horse_cloth` and `reins` parts, the `horse` and `rein_R/L_1..2` bones, `HORSE_*` and `REIN_*`, and `scaled_bones = ["horse"]`.
   - The horse/rein pairs in `CLIP_CHECKS`.
   - In the animate script: `ride()`, `gallop()`, `HORSE_*` and the riding branch of `settle()`.
2. **Add a hover marker** (an effect bone, not mesh): `float_shadow`, a child of `root` on the floor under him. The game can then keep his ring and shadow on the floor while the body is lifted. It may not be needed, because the hips lift and the root stays on the floor; check it in Godot first.
3. Re-run the rest checks, `island_gaps` and the stress poses. Report the triangle count.

## 3. The clips

All clips are 60 fps, generated by script, with every bone keyed on every frame. They're long and theatrical where they're Arts (the owner's preference); hurt stays quick. Times are approximate until each clip is proposed in detail.

### 3.1 Standing and travel

| Clip | Length | Loops | Beats |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `idle` | 3 s | yes | (Already keyed.) Planted. The umbrella is shouldered pike-style on his left pauldron, rising about 5° behind him. His head is tipped 12° to his right so the hat's brim clears it. His right fist is on his hip. He breathes, glances to his right, and the hat drops and cape stir. |
| `move_start` (launch) | ~0.6 s | no | Peach-style. He crouches and springs straight up. At the top of the jump the umbrella swings off his shoulder, his left arm goes out to the side and it **pops open** (ribs out, runner up). He catches under it and hangs. Ends in the `move` pose. |
| `move` (float) | ~1 s | yes | Mary Poppins-style. He hangs from the open umbrella 20 cm off the floor (agreed), the canopy about 1.2 m up and tilted a little toward his heading. A gentle bob and sway, legs dangling and swinging loosely, toes pointed, cape trailing. Authored at the game's travel pace (`move.speed` 1.8 m/s), though nothing needs to match the floor. |
| `hover` | ~1.5 s | yes | The same float, but **stationary**: he hangs in place, drifting a touch, legs idly swinging. Played when he stops moving while still aloft. |
| `move_end` (landing) | ~0.6 s | no | He floats down, touches toe first and settles. The umbrella **snaps shut** (ribs in, runner down) and swings back onto his shoulder. Ends in the `idle` pose. |

**The umbrella arm while floating:** his hat brim is 24 cm wide and sits just above his shoulders, so a hand raised over his head would pass through it. His left arm reaches out to the side, the hand just under the brim's edge, with the shaft rising straight up outside the brim.

## 4. The game side (Godot)

### 4.1 Travel state: launch, float, hover, land

UnitModel and UnitCard today only switch between `idle` and the `move` loop (`UnitCard.set_moving`). The Umbrella needs a small state machine. It applies only to models that have the clips, so other toys are unchanged.

| From | Event | To |
| --- | --- | --- |
| grounded (`idle`) | starts moving | `move_start`, then `move` |
| airborne (`move`) | stops moving | `hover` |
| airborne (`hover`) | starts moving again | `move` (no new launch) |
| airborne (`hover`) | **3 s with no movement** (the owner's rule) | `move_end`, then `idle` |
| airborne (`move` or `hover`) | **the action menu opens for him** (the owner's rule) | `move_end`, then `idle`, at once |

The 3 s count runs on game time (Battle Speed scales it). It resets whenever he moves.

Where it lives: `UnitModel` holds an `airborne` flag and a stationary timer. `play("move")` routes to `move_start` when grounded. Stopping routes to `hover` when airborne, and `_process` starts the landing after `LAND_AFTER` (an `@export`, default 3.0 s).

Travel during the launch: the walk tween starts at once, so he rises as he begins to move. The launch's spring is mostly upward, so starting to slide during it reads fine.

### 4.2 Other clips while he's aloft

He lands first (`move_end`), then the clip plays; see §6. `UnitModel.play(anim)` for any non-travel clip while `airborne` queues it after the landing.

## 6. Decisions (the owner, 2026-10-12)

1. **Attacking or using an Art while aloft:** he lands first (a quick `move_end`), then plays the clip. Every attack and Art starts from the shouldered stance, so no clip needs an airborne version.
2. **All of his actions are from the ground.** Being hit, his turn ending and the Gaze's object form happen only when he's grounded, so they need no airborne versions.
   - As a safety net, anything other than travel that reaches his model while he's still aloft (any clip, `hurt`, `broken`, the Gaze swap) first plays `move_end` (or snaps to the ground if a clip is cut short).
3. **Float height:** about 20 cm off the floor.
4. **The action menu lands him:** if the player opens the action menu (the action ring) while he's still floating, he lands at once with `move_end`, without waiting out the 3 s.
   - So he's on the ground, umbrella shouldered, before the player picks an attack, an Art or Wait.
   - In code, the battle view tells his card when the ring opens for him (`UnitCard.land_now()` → `UnitModel`, a no-op for other toys and when grounded).
5. **Broken's tent:** the `lance` bone moves from `hand_L` to `root`. Every clip keys it into his
   hand each frame, so nothing else looks different. In `broken` he lets go and it lands over him.
6. **Lunge is split at the game's handoffs:** `lunge` (lead-in, ends couched), `lunge_run` (a couched
   run loop at a fixed speed, `lunge_run.speed`, played as long as the dash takes) and `lunge_strike`
   (the spike lands on `hit`, skid, re-shoulder). Its dash plays these clips, never the
   float travel, even though the rules send it as a `move` event.
7. **Raincheck's shield** faces the nearest enemy that can shoot; he turns before planting it.
8. **Raincheck's range** is 2 m from his centre to each ally's centre (one old tile is one metre),
   for both the redirect and the rain circle.
9. **Floating arm:** the shaft tilts a few degrees in toward over his head, still outside the brim.
10. **Launch:** `move_start` stays about 0.6 s, snappy.
11. **Springs:** the hat's drops (`hat_tip_0..7`) get spring bones with the cape and tabard.
